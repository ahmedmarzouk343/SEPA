# Experiment 4: AI chart judge — team plan (for the owner's approval)

**Question.** Our trend/RS filters agree with Minervini (50 of 52 of his disclosed buys pass), but our rule-based VCP detector rejects most of his entry charts, and ~255 stocks pass the filters on a typical day. Can an AI judge the charts, the part he does by eye, better than our rules?

**Status.** This is a plan; nothing is built yet.

## What the team agrees on

1. **Expect little.** Published evidence says off-the-shelf AI reads charts barely better than chance (about 53%). No AI version of Minervini or O'Neil base-reading shows after-cost profits. Simple chart statistics already separate future winners at AUC 0.81 on Fork 4's set, while the classic VCP cues score about 0.51.
2. **Memorisation is the main danger.** Models have read the history of 2017–2025. Hiding tickers and dates is not enough, because market-wide shapes date a chart. Only data after a model's training cutoff is a clean test, and the judge must be a tool-less API call.
3. **The clean data is short.** gpt-oss-120b (text only, open weights, cutoff June 2024, available via our Cerebras/Groq keys) gives about 23 clean months. The newest vision models give 0–10 months.
4. **Overfitting risk.** Prompt, model and format choices multiply to about 9,700 configurations. So: freeze everything before scoring, score once, and never tune on Minervini's trades.
5. **Test at the signal level, not the portfolio.** Judge every candidate chart and compare forward returns of "AI yes" vs "AI no" on the same days. The portfolio makes only ~2 trades a week, far too few to test.

## The plan: four gated steps, each able to stop the project

| Step | What | Calls / cost | Stop if |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Can it read a base at all?** | Synthetic textbook VCPs vs non-VCPs, generated by code (no real stocks, no outcomes) | ~100 calls, free tier | it can't tell a textbook VCP from noise (< 80% correct) |
| **1. Does it cheat?** | Fork 2's leakage probe on anonymised real charts: guess ticker, sector, year, next move — skill before vs after its cutoff | ~760 calls, free tier | pre-cutoff skill is clearly higher than post-cutoff (it remembers) |
| **2. Does it see what Minervini sees?** (descriptive) | First Trial's set, scored on the chart up to the **evening before** his buy; must beat the trivial last-bar baseline | ~140 calls, ≈ $0.25 | it can't pick his buys better than the simple baseline |
| **3. The real test (one shot, frozen)** | Fork 4's post-June-2024 breakouts (374), plus ~1,500 candidate days from our S&P 1500 run in the clean window. Compare the 60-day forward returns of AI-yes vs AI-no, against the mechanical VCP detector and the 0.81 statistics baseline | ~2,000 calls, ≈ $2–5 (a small paid plan, or free tier spread over days) | AI-yes does not beat AI-no **and** the mechanical detector **and** the statistics baseline |

Only if Step 3 passes: build it into the engine (the architect's design, about 45–75 hours) and paper-trade it forward. Every result before that is labelled **exploratory**.

- **Model:** gpt-oss-120b, text input (anonymised OHLCV numbers). It has the most clean data, open weights (no retirement risk) and existing keys. Every answer is cached and never re-asked.
- **Budget:** stays within the project's $10 AI cap, unless the owner raises it.
- **Timeline:** Steps 0–2 in about a day, limited by free-tier rate limits. Step 3 in 1–2 days.
